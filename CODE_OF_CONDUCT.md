# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
Everyone who takes part in GitQuest is welcome. We treat each other with
respect, patience, and kindness, in issues, pull requests, and everywhere
else this project shows up.

## Good behaviour
- Be kind and clear when you give feedback.
- Help new contributors find their feet.
- Give credit where it is due.
- Keep talk on the project, not on the person.

## Not allowed
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic flooding.
- Sharing another person's private information.

## Reporting
If you see behaviour that breaks this code, open an issue marked
"Code of Conduct" or contact the project owner through GitHub
(@Kashan-2912). Reports are read in confidence.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts
that break this code, to keep the project safe for everyone.
